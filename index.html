About CivicSense

CivicSense combines public participation, AI, gamification and voluntary funding for a cleaner city. Citizens report waste in three sectors (residential, commercial, public places), track the cleanup, earn points and badges for verified cleanliness activities, and can donate to authorized waste-management work with a public transparency report.

AI Live Scan uses on-device computer vision to detect littering, vehicles and number plates, with GPS and photo evidence. Every incident, report, point claim and donation needs human verification before it's final.

This version stores data on this device. A live multi-user version needs a server (for example Firebase) and a payment provider for automatic payment confirmation.
